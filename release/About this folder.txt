This is Cento's folder.

Keep it in your Documents folder, named Cento: that's where Cento looks for
it, every time it opens.

What's in it:

  corpus/                 Cento's own Bach chorales (131 of them), from the
                          Bach Chorales Figured Bass dataset (CC BY 4.0).
                          corpus/README.md says where they come from.

  cento.settings.json     Your settings: which chorale folders are on, the
                          seed, and so on. Cento writes it.
  cento.taste.json        Emily's taste: your likes and dislikes.
  cento.emily.json        The pieces you accepted as Emily's own music.
  cento.snapshots.json    Emily's last 30 snapshots, to roll her back.

The cento.* files appear as you use Cento. Delete one to start that part
afresh; delete the whole folder to remove everything Cento keeps.

You can keep other folders of chorales here too (MIDI files), and add them
in Cento's corpora window.

More: https://github.com/nnokes/cento
